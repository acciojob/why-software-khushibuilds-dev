Why I want to become a developer?

I completed my graduation in B.Sc. Biology. After that, I started preparing for Civil Services. During that preparation phase, I realized that I wanted a career based on practical skills, logic, and problem-solving.

While exploring new career paths, I came across a coding challenge from Code Yogi. I had absolutely no computer background, but I successfully completed their 25 steps out of curiosity. That was the moment I fell in love with coding logic.

To learn more, I started exploring FreeCodeCamp, Google, and AI tools on my own. I realized that software development is the perfect field for me.

To build a strong foundation and learn in a structured way, I joined AccioJob. Now, I am completely focused on developing my skills to start my career as a software developer.
